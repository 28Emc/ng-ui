New `ui-context-menu` component: opens on right-click (or `ContextMenu`/`Shift+F10`/arrow keys from the trigger) positioned at the pointer with viewport clamping, `UiContextMenuItem` model with `label`/`icon`/`shortcut`/`danger`/`disabled`/`separator`, `itemSelected` output, WAI-ARIA `menu`/`menuitem` roles, full keyboard navigation (arrows, Home/End, Enter, Space, Escape) and close on outside click.
