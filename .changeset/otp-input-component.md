New `ui-otp-input` component: two-way `value`, configurable `length`, numeric-only or alphanumeric modes, auto-focus, auto-advance while typing, backspace/arrow navigation, paste support, `complete` output when every box is filled, `disabled` state and per-box `aria-label`s in a `role="group"`.
